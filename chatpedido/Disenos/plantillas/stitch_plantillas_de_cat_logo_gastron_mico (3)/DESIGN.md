## Brand & Style
This design system defines an energetic, crave-inducing, modern interface tailored for fast food ordering and swift food delivery. It captures the visceral excitement of street food culture paired with the frictionless efficiency of high-tier mobile utility. 

The aesthetic merges modern flat card modularity with high-impact color blocking and punchy typographic hierarchy. It intentionally rejects sterile corporate fintech aesthetics in favor of a tactile, appetizing warmth. The emotional objective is instantaneous appetite stimulation, speed, trust in fulfillment, and delight at every step from menu browsing to real-time driver tracking.

## Colors
The palette leverages warm, high-chroma tones scientifically proven to evoke appetite and urgency, grounded against crisp, clean surfaces.

- **Primary (`#FF6B00` - Deep Amber Orange):** Drives the primary action pathway, key interactive CTAs, active selection states, and brand focal points.
- **Secondary (`#E53935` - Warm Red-Orange):** Reserved for high-urgency notifications, hot deal tags, discounts, cart badges, and spice/dietary indicators.
- **Tertiary (`#FFC107` - Energetic Warm Gold):** Applied to ratings, loyalty point counters, express delivery badges, and celebratory micro-moments.
- **Neutral Dark (`#1A1A1A`):** Deep charcoal used for primary text, stark icons, and solid dark button states where maximum contrast is required. Avoid pure `#000000` to prevent harsh visual clipping against warm backgrounds.
- **Surface Foundations:** Background canvas sits on soft warm off-white (`#F9F8F6`), providing organic warmth. Elevated content containers sit on crisp pure white (`#FFFFFF`) to make food photography burst with vibrant contrast.
- **Subtle Stroke (`#EAE7E2`):** Low-contrast warm gray perimeter for cards, pill tags, and segmented pickers.

## Typography
The typographic voice pairs the geometric, streetwise attitude of **Space Grotesk** with the ergonomic legibility of **Plus Jakarta Sans**.

- **Display & Headlines (Space Grotesk):** Features condensed geometric proportions, tight tracking, and muscular line heights. Use for dish names, promotional banners, checkout milestones, and bold value-proposition headers.
- **Body & Controls (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Selected for warm, open counters and high legibility at micro sizes on small mobile screens. Used for ingredient lists, caloric disclosures, delivery ETAs, and interactive form fields.
- **Price Tokens:** Explicitly isolated to Space Grotesk to give pricing clear monetary authority and instant scannability across dense menus.

## Layout & Spacing
A 4-column layout governs mobile viewport form factors, expanding to an 8-column layout on tablet surfaces. 

- **Outer Margins:** Fixed at `1rem` (16px) on mobile viewports to maximize screen real estate for rich food imagery, expanding to `1.5rem` (24px) on tablet devices.
- **Rhythm & Grid:** Built upon a compact 4px baseline sub-grid (`0.25rem`). Horizontal scroll shelves for categories and promotions bleed off canvas margins with negative tracking to encourage tactile swiping.
- **Component Padding Scale:** 
  - Standard card interior padding strictly uses `space-lg` (16px).
  - Compact utility rows and horizontal item lists use `space-md` (12px).
  - Chip containers and pill buttons rely on tight vertical padding `space-xs` (4px) with generous horizontal padding `space-md` (12px).

## Elevation & Depth
Depth avoids muddy, dark shadows in favor of ambient, warm-tinted underglows and crisp spatial layer distinction.

- **Base Layer (Level 0):** Background surface canvas (`#F9F8F6`).
- **Interactive Card Level (Level 1):** Solid white surface (`#FFFFFF`) framed with a 1px solid border stroke of `#EAE7E2`. Supported by an ultra-soft ambient drop: `0px 4px 16px rgba(26, 26, 26, 0.04)`.
- **Floating Cart / Sticky Action Bar (Level 2):** Elevated floating modules sit with a high-diffusion warm lift: `0px 8px 24px rgba(255, 107, 0, 0.12)`, grounding high-priority calls to action directly above content streams.
- **Modals & Bottom Sheets (Level 3):** Modal sheets elevate over a 50% opacity neutral wash (`rgba(26, 26, 26, 0.5)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(4px)`). The container uses `0px -8px 32px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.12)` to denote pull-up physics.

## Shapes
The shape language uses moderate roundedness across cards and containers, shifting to full pill geometry for micro-elements, badges, and primary action controls.

- **Standard Surfaces & Cards:** Utilize `rounded-lg` (1rem / 16px) to maintain a soft, friendly silhouette without losing structural alignment with adjacent imagery.
- **Modal Sheets & Food Hero Containers:** Top corners use `rounded-xl` (1.5rem / 24px) to emphasize tactile presentation.
- **Buttons, Tags, Badges & Counter Steppers:** Pure pill style (`border-radius: 9999px`) provides a friendly, thumb-friendly tap target feel characteristic of modern on-demand apps.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** High-contrast full-bleed pill button (`height: 52px`). Background: `#FF6B00`, text: `#FFFFFF`, font: `label-lg`. Pressed state applies a slight scale down (`scale(0.98)`) and deepens color to `#E05E00`.
- **Secondary / Cart Trigger:** High-contrast neutral pill button (`height: 52px`). Background: `#1A1A1A`, text: `#FFFFFF`, featuring dynamic item counter on the left and total price in `price-md` on the right.
- **Tertiary / Outline:** White pill container with 1.5px border `#EAE7E2`, text `#1A1A1A`. Hover/Active: Background fills with `#F9F8F6`.

### Chips & Pill Tags
- **Category Filter Chips:** Horizontal scrolling pills (`height: 36px`). Inactive state: `#FFFFFF` card background with 1px `#EAE7E2` border and `#1A1A1A` text. Active state: `#1A1A1A` fill with `#FFFFFF` text, or `#FF6B00` fill with `#FFFFFF` text for discount-specific filters.
- **Dietary & Modifier Tags:** Miniature pills (`height: 24px`, padding: `2px 8px`, typography: `label-sm`).
  - Spicy / Hot Deal: Surface `#FEEBEB`, text `#E53935`.
  - Vegan / Healthy: Surface `#E8F5E9`, text `#2E7D32`.
  - Bestseller: Surface `#FFF8E1`, text `#D97706`.

### Food Cards
- **Vertical Food Item Card:** White container (`#FFFFFF`), `rounded-lg`, 1px stroke (`#EAE7E2`), clipped 4:3 ratio image at top. Bottom compartment houses dish name in `headline-sm`, 2-line truncated description in `body-sm` (`#706E6B`), and a bottom anchor row presenting price in `price-lg` paired with a circular (+) add button (`size: 36px`, background: `#FF6B00`, icon: `#FFFFFF`).
- **Horizontal Combo Card:** Flat list modular layout. Left-aligned text stack with right-aligned square image (`size: 96px`, `rounded-md`).

### Stepper Quantity Selector
- Pill-shaped container (`height: 36px`, background: `#F9F8F6`, border: `1px solid #EAE7E2`). Houses minus and plus touch targets surrounding a central numerical counter in `Space Grotesk` `headline-sm`. Decrementing to 0 triggers secondary red-orange trash icon.

### Form Inputs & Search Bar
- **Search Bar:** Pill-shaped (`height: 48px`, background: `#FFFFFF`, border: `1px solid #EAE7E2`). Prefix search icon in `#FF6B00`, placeholder text in `body-md` muted (`#9E9B97`).
- **Text Inputs:** Soft container (`height: 52px`, `rounded-md`, background: `#FFFFFF`, border: `1.5px solid #EAE7E2`). On focus: Border transitions to `#FF6B00` with an outer glow of `0px 0px 0px 3px rgba(255, 107, 0, 0.15)`.

### Delivery Status Tracker Bar
- Compact horizontal segmented indicator. Completed phases colored in `#FF6B00` connected by 3px solid amber track lines; pending phases remain in light gray `#EAE7E2`. Rider delivery ETA emphasized in `display-lg-mobile`.
